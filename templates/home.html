Fact check what actually works

Most of it is boring.
That is the good news.

There is no secret. Results come from a short list of dull, well-proven habits you can keep on a normal week. These are the rules the engine inside The Trainer is built on, with the research behind them.

01

Twice a week beats once, every time.

Train each muscle at least twice a week. So two or three days get full-body sessions and four get upper and lower. Never a one-muscle-a-day bro split. The plan is built on the days you can actually protect.

Schoenfeld, Ogborn & Krieger, 2016 (training frequency meta-analysis)
02

Protein is the lever you are not pulling.

The benefit plateaus around 1.6 grams per kilo of bodyweight a day, with 2.2 as the upper end. Aim higher when you are cutting. At 75 kg that is about 120 grams, every day.

Morton et al., 2018, British Journal of Sports Medicine (49 trials)
03

Soreness is not the scoreboard.

Aching only means a movement was new. The real scoreboard is double progression. When every set reaches the top of the rep range, the weight goes up: 2.5 kg on upper-body lifts, 5 kg on lower. Your log tells you the next number.

How the Trainer’s log sets your next target
04

You will not get bulky by accident.

Even a beginner eating in a surplus on purpose gains about 0.6 to 1 percent of bodyweight a month, and not all of it is muscle. Muscle is slow and stubborn. Nobody wakes up huge.

The gain rate the engine plans for, then checks at week 4
05

There is no fat-burning zone.

Fat comes off through a deficit, about 400 to 500 kcal under what you burn, at 0.5 to 1 percent of bodyweight a week. Lift to keep the muscle, walk to spend energy, and let the food do the maths.

Mifflin-St Jeor energy estimate, corrected every two weeks from your 7-day average
06

Sleep is the cheapest supplement.

Under six and a half hours a night, the plan starts you at the bottom of your volume range. Of everything in the tubs, creatine (3 to 5 g a day) has the strongest evidence. Fat burners and BCAAs don’t make the list.

ISSN position stand on creatine, Kreider et al., 2017

Beat one number at a time. That is the whole method. The plan shows its working, so you never have to remember any of this.
