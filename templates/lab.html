Finding I The headline

The supplement is the whole story.

Across 1,000 participants, strength gains separate almost perfectly by regimen. The combined “Both” protocol leads, creatine sits in the middle, and the mass gainer trails far behind — it is a weight product, not a strength one.

The small number beside each bar is the coefficient of variation (CoV) — the spread relative to the average. A lower CoV means more consistent results from person to person, which answers the dataset's own challenge: which regimen gives reliable strength spikes regardless of who takes it.

Finding II The trade-off

Weight and strength pull in different directions.

Plotting each regimen by what it actually delivers makes the design of the data legible. Mass Gainer buys kilograms but little strength. Creatine buys strength with almost no added weight. Both sits in the prized upper-right — gains on each axis at once.

Each point is a regimen's average outcome
Finding III The non-story

Who you are barely moves the needle.

Age, gender, cycle length and starting weight are all but uncorrelated with strength gain — every correlation sits within a hair of zero. Average strength gain is essentially identical across genders, exactly as you'd expect from a field that was assigned at random.

A correlation near zero means the two numbers move independently: knowing someone's age tells you nothing useful about their result. The bars below are drawn on a wide −0.2 to +0.2 scale so you can see just how flat they really are.

Finding IV The model

A model confirms it — one feature does the work.

Predicting strength gain from a participant's profile (age, gender, cycle length, starting weight and supplement), a cross-validated model lands around 3.4 points of average error. The mean-only baseline is roughly twice as wrong, so the features genuinely help — but which ones?

Permutation importance answers that: shuffle the Supplement column and the model collapses; shuffle anything else and almost nothing happens. In other words, the entire predictive signal is the regimen.
